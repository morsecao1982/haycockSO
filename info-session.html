01 · The Competition

What Is Science Olympiad?

Science Olympiad is a national STEM competition where teams of students compete across events spanning life science, earth & space science, physical science, engineering, and inquiry — through written tests, hands-on labs, and built devices.

Division A

Nationally grades K–6 (split A1/A2). At Haycock, our 3rd and 4th graders compete here.

Division B

Nationally grades 6–9 — this is Haycock's team. As a K–6 school with no middle-school grades of our own, our 5th and 6th graders move up into Division B under Science Olympiad's own rule for schools with no in-range team available.

A 5th grader may compete in Division A or Division B — not both in the same year. This is a one-team commitment.

05 · Time & Effort

Not A Drop-Off Activity

Students

At least 2 hours a week, per event, studying together. Complex written events and builds routinely take longer to get good at.

Parents

Every family mentors 1–2 events. That means making sure your kids are prepared — folders and cheat sheets ready, studying and teaching alongside them, running practice tests.

This only works if parents mentor. Please plan for that before you apply.

08 · Communicating Event Preferences

Sorting Into Events

Option A

Rank your top 6 individual events. We match students to events one at a time, based on preference and fit.

Option B

Rank preferred event groups — pre-set pairings of events students take on together as a set.

Which method we use depends on final enrollment numbers and the 5th/6th grade ratio — we'll decide once sign-ups close. Either way, we'll ask for your preferences on the Student Intake Form.

09 · What Happens Next

Interest Isn't The Commitment

Sharing your email with us today just tells us you're interested. Every family who does will receive a Student Intake Form — and completing that is the real commitment to participate.

Before You Fill It Out

It's long, and it asks for a lot — availability, preferences, achievements, parent involvement. We need all of it to sort every student into events fairly. Please take the time to fill it in completely.
