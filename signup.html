Science Olympiad Division B is a middle-school competition featuring challenges in biology, chemistry, engineering, physics, earth science, and technology. It is a great opportunity for students ready for advanced scientific problem-solving, hands-on challenges, and teamwork.

Students in 5th and 6th grades are invited to participate. Fifth graders may also choose Division A, the elementary-school division, which is less complex and demanding; however, students may participate in only one division.
